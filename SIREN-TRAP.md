# A stranger’s request

One-time Outer Realm deception. On eligible new Nature rolls, after checking the existing thief encounter, a 2% roll can reveal the stranger (at most about 0.8% of ordinary Outer encounter rolls; quiet steps and occupied missions reduce the effective chance). Never rolls on discovered ground or the border. Reserves the exclusive side-quest window until declined or escaped.

Accept reserves fresh Outer ground 5–9 cardinal steps away and adds pale pulsing compass guidance, distinct from Grandfather’s gold. Decline reveals her true form with no health or Hope penalty. Entry costs 4 HP. Climbing guarantees escape for 3 HP; searching succeeds at 50%, otherwise costs 2 HP. After three failed searches, the next succeeds. Damage floors at 1 HP with automatic home rescue. No timed poison damage inside the pit. Escape leaves ordinary poison while injured; full healing cures it. Source and destination become stable wilderness after completion.

Version-1 saves default siren to null and sirenDone to false. Validation checks stages, coordinates, tiles, attempts and mission exclusion. Reduced motion makes guidance static.
