A FIELD GUIDE TO STRONGWOOD

How to play Ominous Realms

A free, single-player dark fantasy browser RPG about how far you dare to wander. Choose your weapon, leave your village, and decide when to fight, forage or return home.

Getting startedExplorationCombatHealingForgingPuzzles and lootSaving progress

Begin your journey

Start in Strongwood, accept Eldric’s armor, choose a Sword, Axe, Hammer or Knife, and name your warrior. Each weapon offers different attacks. The game runs on mobile and desktop in a modern browser, with no download or account required.

Explore a world that remembers

Use the compass to move north, south, east or west. Discovered coordinates keep their locations when you return. The Map shows nearby discoveries, including forges and rare puzzle sites. New journeys begin with a cobblestone trail heading north from home. Follow it to the forge, tavern, wizard and herbalist in turn. Each person points you onward. Each stretch winds for three cardinal steps, and the path has no random encounters. Each introduction happens once; the wizard, barkeep and herbalist leave afterward.

Encounters offer actions beneath the compass. Speak with travelers, inspect a find, or enter battle. Choosing a direction lets you pass an encounter and continue walking. Beyond Strongwood’s boundary lies the more dangerous Outer Realm.

Choose your attacks carefully

Each attack displays power and accuracy. Stronger attacks may be less reliable, and either fighter can miss. Your attack resolves before the enemy response. Watch the health cards and colored result toasts; a finishing blow ends the fight without a counterattack.

Visit the wizard on a village path to enchant your weapon and unlock Realmfire. Each use then consumes a Magic Crystal. You can also attempt to flee or spend a Gem to bribe a threat. Defeated enemies leave their coordinates permanently cleared. Victories advance your level, and each new level requires more wins.

Heal with potions or rest at home

Potions restore all missing health. Use one from Supplies in your Pack or the HUD’s healing control. Walking while wounded and without a potion can reveal healing supplies. Mushrooms are a gamble: you will not know their effect before eating them.

Defeat or exhaustion at 1 health returns you to your village home. Resting there gradually restores health, including time spent away from the game. Returning from 1 health to full takes about ten minutes. Leaving home stops that recovery.

Enhance equipment at a forge

Bring Metal Ingots for armor and Steel Ingots for your weapon. Follow a village path to Eldric’s forge. His first welcome includes one Metal Ingot and one Steel Ingot; the forge remains usable afterward. Discovered wilderness forges remain useful stops too. Enter the Forge to see both upgrades and their costs.

The WPN and ARM boxes glow gold when you have enough ingots for the next enhancement. Tapping either box shows that item alone. You must be standing at a forge to upgrade; away from one, you can inspect your equipment and plan your return.

Find rare puzzles and hidden treasure

Some sealed passages open through an offering plate, rotating rune stones or a sequence of levers. Read the inscription and use the controls beneath the compass. Progress survives leaving and returning; opened chambers remain open, and their loot can be gathered once.

Disturbed earth may conceal treasure. Digging costs health, and the remaining depth stays unknown. Decide whether to risk another foot or walk away. When you uncover a cache, choose Gather Loot to add its contents to your pack.

Where is your progress saved?

Your journey saves automatically in this browser on this device. Return using the same browser and site address to continue. Existing journeys from before the village-path update retain their original locations, home forge and magic access. Start a new journey to explore the connected village trail. Saves do not sync between devices; clearing site data, resetting your journey or ending a private browsing session can remove progress.

Is Ominous Realms free?

Yes. Play directly in your browser without a download or account. JavaScript is required to play, and browser storage must be available to keep your save.
