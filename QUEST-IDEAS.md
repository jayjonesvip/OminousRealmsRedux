# Quest ideas for later

These ideas are parked, not implemented.

- **Grandfather's unfinished battle:** defeat a threat the grandfather once failed to stop. Reveal the personal history through encounters and his guidance. Reuse the subtle golden compass glow when appropriate.
- **Let Strongwood respond:** after meaningful deeds, villagers describe concrete improvements: a safe road, recovered supplies, or someone able to return home. Make protecting the village visible through existing dialogue and encounter flows.

Keep the player's freedom to explore; the grandfather points the way without choosing for them.
