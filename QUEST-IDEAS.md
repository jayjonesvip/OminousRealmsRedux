# Quest ideas for later

The grandfather quest chain and its final act are now implemented.

- **Implemented: The Cinderseam Remains.** Rauthkell’s defeat reveals a grimoire, six pursuing guards, Sereth Venn’s Strongwood’s Oath enchantment, and Ordrath’s final battle. The grandfather rests after the Cinderseam falls. See FINAL-ACT.md for behavior and artwork prompts.

- **Implemented: Grandfather’s unfinished battle.** Rauthkell killed the grandfather. This final quest follows the keepsake, watchpost, provisions and ogre quests. The pulsing compass guides the player, and completion explains how each action helped.
- **Implemented: Strongwood remembers.** Quest deeds persist as world flags. Strongwood villagers, farmers, woodcutters and elders first acknowledge newly completed deeds, then occasionally mix those reactions into ordinary conversation. Watchpost stores, returned provisions, the defeated ogre, the reopened road and Rauthkell’s death each get a one-time journal entry. Existing completed quests backfill flags without duplicate entries. No encounter tiles change.

Keep the player's freedom to explore; the grandfather points the way without choosing for them.
