# Quest ideas for later

The grandfather quest chain and its final act are now implemented.

- **Implemented: The Veil Remains.** Hollowflame’s defeat reveals a grimoire, six pursuing guards, Vaelric’s Veilbreaker enchantment, and Malrec’s final battle. The grandfather rests after the Veil falls. See FINAL-ACT.md for behavior and artwork prompts.

- **Implemented: Grandfather’s unfinished battle.** Hollowflame killed the grandfather. This final quest follows the keepsake, watchpost, provisions and ogre quests. The pulsing compass guides the player, and completion explains how each action helped.
- **Implemented: Strongwood remembers.** Quest deeds persist as world flags. Strongwood villagers, farmers, woodcutters and elders first acknowledge newly completed deeds, then occasionally mix those reactions into ordinary conversation. Watchpost stores, returned provisions, the defeated ogre, the reopened road and Hollowflame’s death each get a one-time journal entry. Existing completed quests backfill flags without duplicate entries. No encounter tiles change.

Keep the player's freedom to explore; the grandfather points the way without choosing for them.
