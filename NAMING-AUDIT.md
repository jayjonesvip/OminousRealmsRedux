# Naming audit — Ominous Realms
Reviewed 2026-09-21 against runtime content and indexed web results.

## Result
The names are **not all unused elsewhere**. Several exact or closely related game uses were found. No exact competing game title “Ominous Realms” was found in this search, but that is not proof of exclusivity. Strongwood searches mainly returned unrelated real-world businesses, rather than an established fantasy-game location.

This is a naming/discoverability audit, not trademark clearance. Search indexes cannot cover every released, unpublished, modded, player-created, or non-English game. Ordinary words such as Sword, Potion, Skeleton, Backstab, and The Hunter cannot sensibly be made exclusive.

The coordinated rename below is implemented. The original audit and catalog remain below as historical evidence. Internal save identifiers and artwork filenames remain stable.

## Other overlaps and qualifications
- **Eldric:** recurring Risen NPC name; [Risen 3 overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risen_3%3A_Titan_Lords). Exact first name, different character.
- **Elara:** [Elara Dorne in SWTOR](https://www.swtor.com/holonet/companions/elara-dorne). Shared first name, not the same full character identity.
- **Vaelric:** [developer prototype announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/talha-agha-a497a61aa_gamedevelopment-indiegamedev-unrealengine5-activity-7461361705231724544-JsCn) uses it as a game title. Also found in player handles/tabletop miniature names. No exact “Vaelric, the Ashen Sage” match established.
- **Malrec:** found as a [player character name](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/character/60108156/) and [homebrew Mordheim character](https://whitelabel.worldanvil.com/w/mordheim-dimmypiano/a/malrec-voss-person). A player or homebrew use is not evidence that this is an official franchise character. Exact full boss title not established.
- **Hollowflame:** found as [a Warcraft player name](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/character/us/wyrmrest-accord/hollowflame/) and [an FFXIV player surname](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/character/43202164/). These do not establish an official named boss collision.
- **Gravewing Gargoyle:** Warcraft has [Gravewing/stoneborn gargoyles](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gravewing). Related modifier/family association, not verified exact full label.
- **Ember Fox:** exact creature label in [Totemancer](https://totemancer.com/library/totem/EFX).
- **Silverrun Stream:** shares Silverrun with [Farming Simulator's Silverrun Forest](https://www.farming-simulator.com/mod.php?mod_id=315279). Different full location label.
- **Whispering Glade / Sunlit Clearing:** exact scenery labels in the publisher's [Word Meadow listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/word-meadow/id6761626767). Common descriptive combinations.
- **Reedwater Marsh:** exact label in [Bunnyland world-building guidance](https://bunnyland.dev/guides/world-building-map.html).
- **The Lantern Tavern:** [Roll20 marketplace description](https://marketplace.roll20.net/browse/set/18642/tavern-of-lanterns) uses Lantern Tavern. Common establishment name.
- **Elder Grove:** an [indexed mobile card-game listing](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.plasdjinnhsakndknaheiudcbjhdaaffsg.eldergrovegiramete) uses it; lower-confidence third-party evidence. Also an ordinary place-name phrase.
- **Veilbound Grimoire:** exact term in [independent fantasy world-building](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/idorin-pjmc127/a/axmos-person), not established here as a shipped-game item.
- **Witchcap:** overlaps the word in League of Legends' [Wooglet's Witchcap](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/Wooglet%27s_Witchcap), a hat. Not an exact mushroom-item match.
- **Writhing Briars:** resembles the singular Writhing Briar attack in tabletop D&D; ordinary descriptive wording, not a confirmed exact location match.
- **Ironjaw Ogre:** related Ironjaw/Ironjawz fantasy uses surfaced, but this audit did not establish an exact authored game enemy with this full name.

“No established match” only describes the reviewed search results. It does not mean unique or legally cleared. The Weighted Gate / Turning Stones and Missing Provisions returned descriptive gameplay uses rather than a confirmed matching named quest/puzzle identity.

## Rename direction for review
- Veilbreaker → **Strongwood’s Oath**.
- Earthshaker → **Gravesoil Blow**.
- Gravewing Gargoyle → **Ruinperch Gargoyle**.

Exact-phrase searches for these three proposals did not establish game-content matches. Other replacements should be checked as a set before changing lore, tutorials and saved display text. Do not replace every generic noun with an invented word.
